# LivWorthy 30-Day Multi-Platform Organic Growth Campaign

**Execution Timeline:** 30 Days (Rolling Calendar)  
**Primary Audience:** International mobile professionals, software engineers, finance expats, remote workers, and cross-border relocators (US, UK, Switzerland, UAE, Canada, Germany, Singapore).  
**Tone & Style:** Analytical, empathetic, transparent, math-grounded, zero sensationalism.  
**Brand Identity Palette:**  
- **Primary Navy:** `#102A2E` (Deep institutional trust & authority)  
- **Accent Teal:** `#167D75` (Clarity, financial vitality & calculators)  
- **Card Background:** `#F7F8F5` / `#FFFFFF`  
- **Contrast Text:** `#334D4A` & `#102A2E`  
- **Warning / Highlight:** `#D97706` (Amber) & `#059669` (Emerald surplus)

---

## Campaign Strategic Pillars & Compliance Charter

1. **Zero Fake Metrics or Synthetic Engagement:** No engagement pods, automated bots, purchased followers, or click rings. Growth relies 100% on high-utility financial intelligence that people naturally save, screenshot, and share.
2. **Transparent Mathematical Grounding:** Every post, script, and slide cites verified statutory rules (IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32, NYS/NYC DTF, Florida Constitution, Swiss ESTV / Canton Zurich, UK HMRC).
3. **Frictionless Bridge to Interactive Tools:** Every piece of social content gives an immediate standalone answer in the post itself, then invites the reader to test their own custom numbers on LivWorthy using an uncluttered, privacy-safe UTM link.
