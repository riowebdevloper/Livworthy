## 3. Investigated Candidate Opportunity Topics

These narrower topics address high-intent user decision crossroads where standard financial tools provide zero actionable answers:

### Topic A: NYC $100K: Roommate vs Solo Apartment Cash Flow
- **User Intent:** Deciding whether to sign an individual lease or find a roommate when moving to NYC on six figures.
- **Mathematical Analysis:**
  - Solo HUD 1-Bed FMR ($2,655/mo): 45.0% of take-home pay committed to housing. Discretionary savings: **$1,660/mo** ($19,920/yr).
  - Roommate 2-Bed Share ($1,500/mo share + half utilities): 25.4% of take-home pay. Discretionary savings: **$2,975/mo** ($35,700/yr).
- **Opportunity:** Extra $15,780/year in capital accumulation. Fully funds 2026 Roth IRA ($7,000) and provides $8,780 in additional liquid reserves.
- **Target URL:** Integrated directly into `/guides/nyc-100k#solo-vs-roommate`.

### Topic B: NYC $100K: Landlord 40× Income Guideline vs Market Reality
- **User Intent:** Understanding if prospective tenants can pass property management screening on $100K.
- **Mathematical Analysis:**
  - $100,000 / 40 = **$2,500/month maximum rent**.
  - 2026 HUD Fair Market Rent for New York City is **$2,655**, which slightly exceeds the strict solo 40× threshold.
- **Practical Guidance:** Identifies specific neighborhoods where modern units rent at or below $2,500 (Astoria, Sunnyside, Crown Heights, Bushwick, Upper East Side pre-war studios) or recommends using an institutional guarantor / roommate.
- **Target URL:** Integrated into `/guides/nyc-100k#rent-context`.

### Topic C: Miami $90K: Car Ownership vs Car-Free Monthly Budget
- **User Intent:** Deciding whether to bring or purchase a car when relocating to Miami for a $90K job.
- **Cost Differential:**
  - Greater Miami Car Owner: Auto loan/depreciation ($280) + FL auto insurance ($180) + gas & SunPass ($60) = **$520/month**.
  - Brickell / Downtown Car-Free: Free elevated Metromover + walking + rideshare buffer ($150) = **$150/month**.
  - **Net Difference:** Living car-free frees up **$370/month** ($4,440/year), increasing savings from $2,272/mo to $2,642/mo.
- **Target URL:** Integrated into `/guides/miami-90k#car-vs-car-free`.

### Topic D: NYC vs Miami Purchasing Power Parity
- **User Intent:** Weighing a job relocation offer between New York City ($100K) and Miami ($90K).
- **Mathematical Analysis:**
  - NYC $100K gross yields **$5,907/month net** ($70,879/yr) after federal, NY state (4.86%), NYC local (3.44%), and FICA.
  - Miami $90K gross yields **$6,012/month net** ($72,145/yr) due to 0% Florida state personal income tax.
  - Miami delivers $105/month **more take-home cash** despite a $10,000 lower gross salary, while median 1-bed rent is ~$500/mo cheaper ($2,150 vs $2,655).
- **Target URL:** Cross-linked between `/guides/nyc-100k`, `/guides/miami-90k`, and `/compare/new-york-vs-miami`.

### Topic E: Zurich CHF 130K: 3-Pillar Architecture & Savings Velocity
- **User Intent:** Global tech/finance professionals evaluating a Swiss Franc salary offer against UK or US benchmarks.
- **Key Realities:**
  - Pillar 1 (AHV/IV/EO/ALV): Mandatory 6.4% deduction (8,320 CHF).
  - Pillar 2 (BVG): Illustrative statutory-minimum employee deduction of 3,213 CHF for age 35–44 (5.0% on coordinated salary).
  - Pillar 3a: Statutory 2026 maximum deduction of **7,258 CHF** (BSV), generating ~1,600 CHF immediate tax savings.
  - Health Insurance (LAMal): Paid out-of-pocket directly to insurers (~380 CHF/mo), not withheld from payroll.
  - **Capital Accumulation:** Clean uncommitted monthly surplus of **4,239 CHF** ($4,800+ USD), exceeding $50,000 annual wealth accumulation.
- **Target URL:** Integrated into `/guides/zurich-130k#wealth-accumulation`.
