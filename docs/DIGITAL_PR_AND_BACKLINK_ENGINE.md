## 1. Ethical Standards & Operating Rules

LivWorthy STRICTLY prohibits:
- Paid link networks, PBNs, or sponsored link farms.
- Automated blog spam or generic directory submissions.
- Deceptive anchor text schemes or fake guest posts.

All links must be earned through:
1. **Original Data & Empirical Research**: Derivative findings from government statistics, housing benchmarks, and statutory tax schedules.
2. **Deterministic Calculators**: Utilities offering superior user experience, zero ads, and strict mathematical auditability.
3. **High-Value Relocation Intelligence**: Cross-border relocation corridors answering complex tax and cost trade-offs.

## 3. Five Original Data Research Studies

### Study 1: The $100K Purchasing Power Paradox (Global Metro Comparison)
- **Core Finding**: On a $100,000 USD gross salary, an individual in New York City retains $19,919/year in discretionary savings after statutory taxes and essential living costs, compared to $44,280 in Austin (TX) and $42,100 in Dubai (AED equivalent).
- **Primary Source Data**: IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-38, NYS IT-2105-I, HUD FY2026 FMR, BLS CEX Table 26-12-BOS.
- **Citation Format**: *LivWorthy Global Metro Purchasing Power Index (2026)*.

### Study 2: Cross-Border Tax Gaps for Mid-Career Software Engineers
- **Core Finding**: Moving from London (£95,000) to New York ($140,000) yields a 22% higher nominal salary but increases combined income and local payroll tax liabilities by 7.4 percentage points, requiring a minimum $152,000 salary to achieve equal savings.
- **Primary Source Data**: HMRC Income Tax Act 2007, NYS DTF Form IT-201, ONS Private Rental Price Index.

### Study 3: The True Cost of Rent in Major Financial Capitals
- **Core Finding**: In 2026, housing costs consume 48.2% of take-home pay for a single earner making $80,000 in Manhattan, compared to 36.1% in Toronto, 38.4% in London (Inner Zone), and 31.2% in Berlin.
- **Primary Source Data**: HUD Fair Market Rents 2026, Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC), ONS UK.

### Study 4: Family vs Single Living Cost Multipliers Across Global Hubs
- **Core Finding**: The cost ratio between a Family of 4 and a Single Earner ranges from 1.78x in Dubai (heavily influenced by private schooling benchmarks) to 2.34x in New York City (driven by childcare and multi-bedroom residential premiums).
- **Primary Source Data**: NYS OCLS Childcare Market Rate Survey, KHDA Dubai Education benchmarks.

### Study 5: Data Freshness and Inflation Lag in International COL Indices
- **Core Finding**: Outdated benchmark indices underestimate current metro living expenses by up to 14.8% due to unadjusted 2022–2024 survey baselines. LivWorthy's automated freshness audit tracks 40 benchmarks with statutory version pinning.
- **Primary Source Data**: LivWorthy Freshness Audit Engine (October 2026).

## 6. Backlink Database Tracking Schema

To maintain strict operational tracking of backlink development, LivWorthy uses the following relational tracking model:

```sql
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS backlink_monitoring (
  id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
  referring_domain VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL,
  source_page_url TEXT NOT NULL,
  target_landing_url TEXT NOT NULL,
  anchor_text VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL,
  acquisition_category VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL, -- 'ORIGINAL_RESEARCH', 'RESOURCE_PAGE', 'EDITORIAL_PR', 'UNLINKED_BRAND_MENTION'
  domain_authority_tier VARCHAR(16) NOT NULL, -- 'TIER_1_GOV_EDU_MAJOR_MEDIA', 'TIER_2_INDUSTRY_PRESS', 'TIER_3_RELEVANT_NICHE'
  link_rel_attribute VARCHAR(32) DEFAULT 'dofollow', -- 'dofollow', 'nofollow', 'ugc'
  first_detected_at TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW(),
  last_verified_at TIMESTAMPTZ DEFAULT NOW(),
  http_status INTEGER DEFAULT 200,
  link_status VARCHAR(32) DEFAULT 'ACTIVE', -- 'ACTIVE', 'LOST', 'REDIRECTED', 'NOINDEX'
  outreach_contact_ref TEXT
);
```
